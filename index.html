Let me say it before you have to

Nobody is worried about you. That is the problem.

You are the one people phone when it falls apart. You have carried men through things that would have finished them. You are steady, and everybody has quietly decided that means you are fine.

So when it is your turn, there is no queue at your door. There is just you, sitting in the car a minute longer than you need to, working out the numbers before you walk in and greet everybody like a man with no problems.

You have read the books. You have done a course or two. You know what you should be doing. And you still lie there at half past one going over the same thing, and by the time the sun comes up you have put the face back on.

It is not weakness. You have simply never had one room where you were allowed to stop performing. Not one. That is all this is.

Before you worry about it

I have never judged a man yet and I am not going to start with you.

I do not care what you drink, what you have done, what you are still doing, or how far away from where you should be you think you are.

Every person is walking their own road at their own pace, and it has never been my job to grade anybody. I have sat with men in the middle of things they were sure would make me get up and leave. I did not leave. Nothing you say to me is going to change how I look at you.

And this is real life, not religion. We deal with your money, your marriage, your work, your temper, your grief and the Monday morning you are dreading. Whatever you believe or do not believe is yours, and it stays yours.

What actually changes

You stop managing it and start moving.

Not motivation. Motivation is finished by Thursday. This is what men tell me is different.

You make the decision

The one you have been circling for months or years. The business, the job, the move, the thing you keep putting off until it decides itself. You make it, and you know why.

You have the conversation

The one with your wife, your father, your business partner or your son, that you have been avoiding because you do not know how it will go. You have it, and it does not blow up.

You stop taking it home

The pressure stays at work instead of coming through the front door with you. Your family gets you back, not the tired short version of you.

You say it out loud

The thing you have never told anybody gets said, in a room where nothing bad happens afterwards. Most men have never had that once in their lives.

You name what is actually driving it

The anger, the avoiding, the exhaustion. Underneath it there is usually one thing, and once a man can name it he stops fighting five problems that were only ever one.

You leave with a plan you will keep

Not a ten year plan. Ninety days, written down, in your own words, made of things you have actually agreed to do.

How I work

I will not hand you a system. I will show you what you cannot see from inside it.

Generic advice is just what worked for somebody else. It fits their life and not yours, and you drop it the first week things get hard. So I will push on what you are avoiding, name the thing you keep walking around, and challenge the story you have been telling yourself about why it cannot change.

You leave every session with one clear action, in writing. Not homework. One thing you actually do before I see you again. And when something out of my own life is useful you get it, as a light and never as a map.

Before you ask

The questions men actually ask me.

What does it cost?

One conversation is R2 000. The Room is R12 000 for twelve weeks, or R21 000 for six months. Payment plans are available. The first twenty minute call costs you nothing, and if you start with one conversation and then carry on, what you paid comes off the price.

What happens on the first call?

You talk and I listen. That is most of it. Near the end I will ask you a question or two, and then I tell you honestly whether I am the right man for this. If I am not, I will say so.

I have tried this before and nothing changed.

Most men who sit down with me have. Usually they were handed somebody else's system and told to fit into it. We start from your actual life instead, and we go at the thing underneath rather than the thing on the surface.

I do not have time for this.

You already spend more than ninety minutes a week going over it in your head at one in the morning. This is the same time, spent somewhere it actually moves.

Do we have to meet in person?

No. Most of it happens on a call, so it works wherever you are in the country. In person in Pretoria North if you would rather.

What if I break down?

Then you break down. It will not be the first time that has happened in front of me and I am not going to rush you through it. That is the point of having one room where you do not have to hold it together.

How long before something is different?

Something usually shifts in the first conversation, because saying it out loud changes it. The rest takes as long as it takes, and I will not sell you a timeline I cannot keep.

Is this confidential?

Yes. What you say to me stays with me.
