How to use this

Message him the same day

He just told a stranger's website the truth about his week. That window closes fast. One honest WhatsApp on the same day beats a perfect one next week.

Lead with what he wants, not what he scored

Open with the thing he said he wants in ninety days. It is on his card. He will know you actually read it.

If it says he is aiming at the wrong wall, say so gently

That is the whole reason to talk. Not "you are wrong". Something like: you said you want Monday to stop undoing Sunday, but reading your answers, the heavier thing looks like carrying it with nobody to tell. Am I close.

Do not coach on WhatsApp

Get him to the twenty minute call. The message is the door, not the room.
