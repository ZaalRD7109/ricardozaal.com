Is this even for you

This is for you if
  • You are tired of being fine in public and flat at home.
  • You have already tried to sort it on your own.
  • You would rather talk to a man who has been in it than read another book about it.
This is not for you if
  • You want a quick fix in one conversation.
  • You want somebody to agree with you. I will not.
  • You need treatment for a diagnosed condition. I am a coach and a pastor, not a therapist, and I will say so rather than take your money.

The things men ask me
