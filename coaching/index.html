Who is talking to you

I am Ricardo Zaal. I have pastored for twenty six years in Pretoria North.

On 10 July 2010 I lost my two sons, my mother and my stepfather. Two days. I know what it is to sit in a room full of people who care about you and want every single one of them to leave.

I have been broke. I have chased money for eighteen years thinking it was the answer. I have spent thousands on courses looking for the thing that would finally make me enough. I have stood in a church foyer and told people I was blessed while I was working out how to reach month end.

So I am not going to preach at you, and I am not going to hand you a system. I have been where you are, and I am not shocked by anything you are about to tell me.

How I talk to a man in pain

This is what I sent to my own brother the day he was breaking. It is exactly how I work.

Ek gaan nie preek en se hoe goed God is nie, maar ek vra wees mens. Huil as jy moet. Skreeu as jy moet. Vloek God as jy moet. Doen net dit wat jou beter gaan laat voel. I am not going to preach and tell you how good God is. I am asking you to be human. Cry if you must. Scream if you must. Swear at God if you must. Just do the thing that is going to make you better.

Be clear on what this is

  • This is paid work. What it costs, we talk about on the call.
  • I take five men at a time. That is all. When it is full, it is full.
  • Between sessions you can send me one message a week and I answer within two working days.
  • I do not do advice. I ask questions until you hear your own answer, because that is the one you will actually keep.
